Useful Information

  • ZZT's high score input only allows names to be 34 characters in length.
  • Names up to 43 characters in length will be displayed, though they will leak outside of the message window.
  • Characters after the 43rd are not written, though the high score file format reserves 50 characters for each name.
  • Scores with blank names do not display, but are considered when adding new high scores.
  • Scores don't have to be in descending order! New high scores are placed above the first score they equal or surpass.
  • Special characters are not supported by this editor, and may not import/export properly.
  • Consult the Wiki of ZZT page on High scores for more information.
